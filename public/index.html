ghost whisperer /noun/

the three areas

Pointspective maps the craft of AI communication across three distinct domains of mastery.

area 01Intuition

Reading the model. Feeling the current of context. Knowing what to ask before you know how to ask it.

area 02Precision

The architecture of the prompt. Every word placed with intent. Structure that leaves no room for drift.

area 03Imagination

Seeing what the machine can become. Unlocking outputs beyond the obvious — where fluency meets invention.
