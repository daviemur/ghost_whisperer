Pointspective

Identifying those with an exceptional gift for AI communication. Providing honest, structured feedback for those who want to know where they stand.

what we do

Pointspective is a benchmarking and ranking service. We assess how people communicate with AI models — not whether they use the right tools or follow the right tutorials, but whether they possess a natural or developed fluency that produces results others cannot.

We are not educators. We do not offer training. We identify, we assess, and we report — with precision and without flattery. Those who score well will know it clearly. Those who do not will receive feedback that is direct and useful.

Pointspective will not have something for everyone. That is by design.

ghost whisperer
ghost whisperer /noun/

One who is exceptionally persuasive and influential on AI models — and efficient in this task. A practitioner who speaks to the machine's nature, shaping its output through mastery of language, intent, and framing.

the three areas
area 01 Intuition

Reading the model. Feeling the current of context. Knowing what to ask before you know how to ask it.

area 02 Precision

The architecture of the prompt. Every word placed with intent. Structure that leaves no room for drift.

area 03 Imagination

Seeing what the machine can become. Unlocking outputs beyond the obvious — where fluency meets invention.
