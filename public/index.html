Definition

Pointspective

Pointspective is a benchmarking and ranking service for AI communication. It assesses how people reason, communicate, and influence AI models when information is incomplete and the stakes are real.

Ghost Whisperer is a practitioner who is exceptionally persuasive and influential with AI models, shaping the model's output through precision, intuition, and strategic communication.

Digital Empathy

Understanding how AI systems process information, where they fail, and how to adjust your communication accordingly.

Logic & Reason

Structured thinking under incomplete information. Building sound arguments and distinguishing evidence from assumption.

Direct Communication

Precision, economy, and clarity. Saying exactly what you mean with no wasted words.

Indirect Communication

Strategic framing and adaptive dialogue. Extracting information without demanding it.

The AI

In each test, you interact with Claude Sonnet 4.5 playing a specific character — an AI candidate reflecting on consciousness, a party in a liability case, or an assistant in a high-stakes problem.

You

This assessment is designed for those using AI in professional or career-minded contexts where precision and strategic thinking matter.

This test might not be for you if:

  • You're exploring AI casually rather than professionally
  • You're better at showing than explaining
  • You're loose with grammar, structure, or written precision

We're upfront about our goal: to identify exceptional ability. Most people don't pass. The benchmark exists to distinguish those who genuinely understand AI communication from those who merely use it.

The Judge

An LLM-based evaluator scores your reasoning, not your fluency. Conclusions without shown work, and confidence without evidence, score low.

Pass: average of 75% or higher, with no dimension below 50%.

More About Our Benchmarking

Process-Based Evaluation

We score the quality of your reasoning path, not just whether you reached a conclusion. A correct answer arrived at through poor logic scores lower than sophisticated reasoning that reaches a valid conclusion through evidence and structure.

Model Baseline Calibration

Each test area has been completed by AI models operating at their competent best. If your performance is indistinguishable from a well-prompted language model, you haven't demonstrated the human edge this benchmark is designed to isolate.

Adaptive Learning

The Judge records unexpected solutions, creative approaches, and novel framings that scenario designers didn't anticipate. These become calibration data, ensuring the assessment evolves as the landscape of AI communication changes.

What We Don't Test

Template application. Rote prompt engineering. Following checklists. These are valuable skills, but they're not what separates users who truly understand AI systems from those who've merely learned to mimic them.

What We Do Test

Situational reading. Adaptive reasoning under uncertainty. The capacity to work with incomplete information. Strategic communication that extracts insight without demanding it. Evidence-rich judgment under real pressure.

The benchmark exists because the difference between using AI tools competently and understanding how they actually behave is not small. It matters.
