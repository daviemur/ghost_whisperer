THE PROBLEM

WHAT IS THE CHINOOK?
THE WORLD'S MOST EFFICIENT
3-PERSON VEHICLE.
A 500W axial flux electric pedicab built on a bamboo-basalt-carbon chassis. Open source. Community designed. Targeting Vancouver's Vehicles for Hire certification — and beyond.
1
PCB STATOR MOTOR

130mm axial flux design — 70% lighter than traditional radial motors. Zero magnetic cogging means effortless pedalling and regenerative braking.

2
BAMBOO CHASSIS

Structural bamboo reinforced with basalt-carbon hybrid wrap. High impact toughness, vibration dampening, and stiffness — from sustainable materials.

3
LIVING NERVOUS SYSTEM

Built-in Structural Health Monitoring tracks stress at joints in real-time. The frame reports its own health to prove safety to city engineers.

4
FULLY OPEN SOURCE

Designed with UBC BAM Lab. Every file documented transparently. Built for the global DIY and sustainable transport community.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
▼

Motor — Chinook Alpha Rev 1.0

  • 500W axial flux PCB stator, 130mm diameter
  • 4oz copper trace power stage
  • Zero cogging, regenerative braking, no-drag coasting
  • Designed in Flux PCB Editor

Chassis

  • Structural bamboo pole frame (Coolite Bamboo, Vancouver)
  • Basalt-carbon hybrid wrap for impact toughness and stiffness
  • 500kg payload capacity

Structural Health Monitoring

  • AD8226 instrumentation amplifiers
  • ADS1115 ADCs for real-time joint stress monitoring
  • Data for Vancouver Vehicles for Hire certification
